# Apollo Commercial Lead Generation Pipeline

## Project Overview

A commercial exterior cleaning company wanted to expand into new industries but needed a more consistent way to identify qualified decision-makers. I developed and refined Apollo search strategies to identify facilities managers, maintenance managers, regional maintenance managers, property managers, and other commercial contacts across multiple industries.

The project focused on creating a repeatable lead generation process that reduced manual research while improving lead quality for commercial outreach.

---

## Business Challenge

Commercial prospecting often produced inconsistent results because search criteria varied between industries and organizations. Many searches returned contacts who were not responsible for hiring exterior maintenance vendors.

The objective was to build a repeatable process that consistently identified qualified decision-makers while reducing duplicate work.
