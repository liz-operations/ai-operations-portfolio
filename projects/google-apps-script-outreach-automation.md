# Google Apps Script Outreach Automation

## Project Overview

A commercial exterior cleaning company needed a more efficient way to manage outreach to property managers and commercial prospects. The existing process relied on manually importing contact lists, sending emails individually, and tracking outreach in spreadsheets.

I helped build and refine a Google Apps Script workflow that connected Apollo lead exports, Google Sheets, Gmail, and AI-assisted email drafting into a repeatable outreach process. The goal was to reduce repetitive administrative work, improve consistency, and make outreach easier to track and scale.

Throughout this project, I tested workflows, modified automation logic, improved spreadsheet organization, documented processes, and helped troubleshoot issues as the system evolved.

## The Business Challenge

Commercial outreach involved several manual steps:

- Organizing contact lists
- Tracking outreach status
- Preparing personalized emails
- Avoiding duplicate contacts
- Sending emails in manageable batches
- Keeping records up to date

As the volume of contacts increased, manually managing these tasks became increasingly time-consuming and difficult to maintain consistently.

## Lessons Learned

This project reinforced that successful automation is about much more than writing code. Building reliable operational workflows requires careful planning, testing, documentation, and continuous refinement to ensure the process remains accurate, scalable, and easy for others to use.
