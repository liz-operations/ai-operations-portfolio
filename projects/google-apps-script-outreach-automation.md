# Google Apps Script Outreach Automation

## Project Overview

This project focused on improving a commercial outreach workflow by reducing manual work and creating a more repeatable process for personalized email campaigns.

Working with Google Workspace tools, I helped develop and refine an automation workflow that connected contact data, email templates, and Google Apps Script to support efficient outreach.

---

## The Business Challenge

Commercial outreach involved several manual steps:

- Organizing contact lists
- Tracking outreach status
- Preparing personalized emails
- Avoiding duplicate contacts
- Sending emails in manageable batches
- Keeping records up to date

As the volume of contacts increased, manually managing these tasks became increasingly time-consuming and difficult to maintain consistently.

---

## My Contributions

I contributed to the design, testing, and refinement of the workflow by:

- Organizing and validating contact data
- Testing Google Apps Script automations
- Troubleshooting workflow issues
- Helping improve batch email processes
- Identifying duplicate contacts before outreach
- Coordinating data between Google Sheets, Gmail, and Apollo
- Using AI tools to improve communication drafts and workflow efficiency

## Lessons Learned

This project reinforced that successful automation is about much more than writing code. Building reliable operational workflows requires careful planning, testing, documentation, and continuous refinement to ensure the process remains accurate, scalable, and easy for others to use.

It also strengthened my understanding of how AI tools and automation can complement human decision-making by reducing repetitive work while allowing people to focus on higher-value tasks.
